            <p class="pi-lede">Xianshu Bai is a Professor at China Pharmaceutical University and a recipient of the National Overseas High-level Young Talents Program. Her research focuses on how neuroglial cells regulate neural networks under physiological and pathological conditions, with systematic discoveries on oligodendrocyte-mediated neural circuit regulation. As corresponding author, she has published over 10 papers in journals including <em>Developmental Cell</em>, <em>Nature Communications</em> (2), and <em>Glia</em>. She has led more than 10 projects funded by NSFC, DFG, DAAD, and related programs. Her awards include the Du Bois-Reymond Prize and the Alois Lauer Medical Award. She also serves as a grant reviewer for ISN, an invited reviewer for UKRI, a committee member for DFG SPP2561, and a member of the organizing committee for the 2027 European Meeting on Glial Cells.</p>
