            <p class="pi-lede">Ziyang Zhang is a Professor and Doctoral Supervisor in the School of Pharmacy at China Pharmaceutical University. He is a recipient of the National Natural Science Foundation of China's Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas) and has been selected for the university's Leading Scholar (Class A) Talent Program. His research focuses on the pathogenic mechanisms of neurodegenerative diseases and targeted drug discovery within the protein quality control (PQC) system. Centered on the unbalanced aggregation and clearance of aberrant proteins such as Aβ and Tau in Alzheimer's and Parkinson's diseases, he uses chemical biology, cryo-electron microscopy, high-throughput screening, and gene editing to reveal proteostasis mechanisms and develop new therapeutic strategies. His first-author work in <em>Science</em>, <em>Science Signaling</em>, and <em>PLOS Biology</em> provides new perspectives on neurodegenerative disease progression.</p>
