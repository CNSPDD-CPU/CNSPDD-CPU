            <p>On the morning of June 11, 2026, the Center for Neuroscience and Precision Drug Discovery (CNSPDD) held an Open Day event on the fifth floor of the Biology Building. The event was organized for undergraduate students from relevant disciplines, aiming to help them gain a closer understanding of the Center’s research environment, experimental platforms, and major research directions, as well as the interdisciplinary field of neuroscience and precision drug discovery.</p>
<p>During the event, undergraduate student representatives visited Laboratories. Faculty members and graduate student representatives introduced the functional layout of the laboratories, the development of research platforms, and ongoing scientific work at the Center. They also presented the Center’s research foundations and distinctive strengths in cell and molecular biology techniques, neuroscience research platforms, and precision drug discovery. Through on-site explanations and laboratory visits, the students gained a more direct understanding of the research environment, commonly used experimental techniques, and the general process of conducting scientific research projects.</p>
<p>During the visit, faculty members and graduate students also exchanged views with the students on research training, selection of research directions, and preparation for graduate-level study, and answered questions raised by the participants. The Open Day provided undergraduate students with an opportunity to approach research platforms, learn about the Center’s development, and experience its academic atmosphere, laying a foundation for their future participation in research training and graduate study.</p>
<p>In the future, CNSPDD will continue to promote the opening of research platforms and strengthen communication between faculty and students. By leveraging its experimental platforms and interdisciplinary strengths, the Center will provide a supportive learning and research environment for students interested in neuroscience, drug discovery, and related interdisciplinary fields.</p>
