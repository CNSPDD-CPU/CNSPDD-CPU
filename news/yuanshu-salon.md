            <p>On June 24, 2026, the International Academic Salon of the Yuanshu Brain Science Research Center was successfully held. Leading scholars in brain science from China, South Korea, Germany, and other countries gathered to exchange views and engage in academic discussions on the critical roles of glial cells in brain aging and neurological diseases. Several PIs from the Center for Neuroscience and Innovative Drug Development (CNSIDD) participated in the salon and held in-depth discussions with invited experts on related frontier scientific topics.</p>
<p>Professor Frank Kirchhoff, Member of the Academia Europaea and Director of CNSIDD, attended the event. Professor Xianshu Bai and Professor Lei Ma, both PIs of CNSIDD, served as moderators of the academic salon. CNSIDD PIs Professor Yuanqing Feng, Professor Yifei Zhou, Professor Ziyang Zhang, and Professor Pin Li also participated in the discussions. The attending experts exchanged views on glial cell function, mechanisms of brain aging, the development of neurological diseases, and potential intervention strategies, sharing recent progress and academic insights in the field.</p>
<p>The international academic salon provided a high-level platform for exchange between CNSIDD PIs and experts in brain science from China and abroad. Through face-to-face academic discussions, participants deepened their understanding of glial cell biology, mechanisms of brain diseases, and precision intervention research, laying a solid foundation for future interdisciplinary collaboration and international academic exchange.</p>
<p>CNSIDD will continue to focus on frontier questions in neuroscience and the needs of precision drug discovery. The Center will actively participate in and promote high-level international academic exchange, strengthen connections with leading research teams at home and abroad, and further expand its cooperation network and academic influence in the interdisciplinary field of brain science and drug discovery.</p>
