    <div class="news-article-content">
        <div class="news-article-copy">
            <p>On October 24, 2026, the Center for Neuroscience and Innovative Drug Development (CNSIDD) at China Pharmaceutical University organized a team hiking activity along the &quot;Rose Route&quot; in the Tangshan–Fangshan area of Jiangning District, Nanjing. The activity offered center members an opportunity to enjoy nature, engage in physical exercise, and strengthen connections beyond their daily research activities.</p>
            <p>The highlight of the hike was its distinctive rose-shaped route, with the GPS track forming the outline of a rose on the map. This creative design added a memorable element to the outdoor experience. Along the way, participants enjoyed the autumn scenery, exchanged thoughts on work and daily life, and encouraged one another throughout the hike. Photographs captured memorable moments of the activity.</p>
            <p>Scientific research requires not only dedication and rigorous thinking but also physical well-being and effective teamwork. The hiking activity provided an opportunity for informal communication, helping to strengthen mutual understanding, team cohesion, and a sense of belonging. CNSIDD will continue to organize team-building activities that encourage communication and collaboration, fostering an open, supportive, and dynamic research environment.</p>
